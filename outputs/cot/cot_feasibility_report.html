Read this number carefully

pooled out-of-fold over 5 folds (n=1752). Pooled over all folds, but greedy single-chain and crafted exemplars — still a feasibility result, not a tuned benchmark. DAIC-WOZ is public, so Qwen may have seen related data — a strong score is not automatically a clean win. Encoder = its OOF slice on the same folds (full 5-fold pooled OOF macro-F1 was 0.348).

Next steps if GO/PROMISING: (1) add self-consistency (sample N chains, majority vote) for calibrated probabilities; (2) use a strong teacher to generate rationales over the train folds; (3) distill into a small open model that runs offline on the 3090s.
