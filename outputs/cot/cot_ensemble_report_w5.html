CoT + encoder ensemble — PHQ-8 item severity

Combining CoT (Qwen-7B) and MentalBERT+CORN on the paired 5-fold OOF (n=1752). Ensembles use no extra training; nested-CV blend selects the weight leakage-free.

TL;DR — an ensemble beats BOTH parents on macro-F1 and QWK

Severity-routing (use CoT's call when it predicts a severe class ≥2, else the encoder) tops every method on macro-F1 (0.377) and QWK (0.379) — beating both parents at once — and gives the best severe-class F1. Its cost is MAE (0.740, worst): when it trusts CoT on a severe call that's wrong, the miss is larger. If macro-F1 / ordinal agreement / catching severe cases is the goal, this is the config to carry forward; if minimising absolute severity error matters most, the prob-average blend recovers the encoder's MAE (0.735) and the highest accuracy (0.501) while still beating the encoder on macro-F1.
